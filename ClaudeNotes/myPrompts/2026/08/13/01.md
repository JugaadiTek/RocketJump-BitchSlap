  - Improve the agro and pathfinding of the slug projectilles after they touch the ground.

- New Weapon: Black Hole Gun
  - Shoots a projectile that creates a black hole on impact.
  - The black hole will pull in all players and projectiles within a certain radius.
  - The black hole will last for 10 seconds before dissipating.
  - Players caught in the black hole will take damage over time.
- New Weapon: Brambles Cannister Grenade Launcher
  - Shoots a grenade in an arc that explodes into a rapidly growing chaotically asymetrical large patch of thorny brambles.
  - The brambles will deal damage over time to players within its radius.
  - The brambles will last for 60 seconds before dissipating.
  - Players caught in the brambles will have their vision obscured and movement speed reduced.
